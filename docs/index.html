About Project

WHAT IS THE WHITE RAY TOKEN?

White Ray Token is the world’s first cryptocurrency project created for professionals whose job is to help people become healthier and happier. It is a utilitarian and investment token built on the TON blockchain. You can invest your funds in it to generate income, and it can be used to participate in the Association’s community and its programs. White Ray Token is not a currency; rather, it is a digital asset—a form of digital property. Like a physical object, it can be bought, sold, gifted, or bequeathed. We anticipate its value will grow over time as the Association’s community expands and continues to invest in it on a monthly basis. To join the Association, you must own a certain amount of White Ray Tokens, similar to joining an exclusive club.

Details on how to buy the token, the conditions for joining the Association, why it is safe and profitable to purchase, and the main features of the token can be found below in the FAQ section. If you are committed to helping people and the planet, we would be thrilled to have you join the Association. Together, we can achieve so much more!

Members of the Association who wish to do so can post advertisements and specialist profiles on the Association’s website, making it easier for others to access their help and services. There is no fee to join the Association; you simply need to own a small amount of White Ray Tokens. Purchased tokens can be placed in farming or staking projects, where they will generate daily income in the form of interest on your deposit. If desired, White Ray Tokens can easily be sold at market value on the DEDUST.IO exchange. For more information about the Association’s current programs, please visit our official Telegram channels and YouTube channel.

This is not a memecoin but a long-term project designed to evolve and improve over the years.

Staking

What is staking and how does it work?

Staking is a process in which a user freezes his tokens on a smart contract for a certain period of time and receives a reward in the form of interest on his deposit. Staking can be compared to a bank deposit, where the smart contract acts as a bank instead of a bank, guaranteeing the safety of the funds and accruing returns.

In WHR staking, there are several anstake periods (30, 90 and 180). These are measured in days and indicate how long a staker needs to wait between sending a request for a free withdrawal and the opening of a withdrawal opportunity.

It is important to realize that the period is not counted from the moment the tokens are blocked, but from the moment the withdrawal request is sent. Thus, if you have blocked tokens with an unstake period of 30 days, and you send a request six months after making a deposit, you will still need to wait 30 days for a free withdrawal. No rewards will be given for the tokens requested for withdrawal.

ATTENTION: In case you need to withdraw the tokens from staking urgently, instant withdrawal is also available in our staking pool, but it will incur a 5% COMMISSION + 0.3 TON fee.

So be careful when choosing a method of withdrawal from staking!

Why do I need to make a deposit to the token liquidity pool?

The token is bought and sold on the DEX exchange, the exchange on which the token is traded (DEDUST.IO) takes a commission (0.25%) for each buy and sell transaction. 70% of this commission is distributed to all people who have contributed liquidity to the liquidity pool. You can also contribute your liquidity (consisting of White Ray token (WHR ) and TON) to the pool and accumulate a percentage of the commission. When you decide to withdraw your liquidity from the pool, you will receive your invested funds plus the amount accumulated as a percentage of the exchange’s commission payments. The percentage depends on the number of trades that take place on the exchange each day and the White Ray token management team cannot influence this.

 IMPORTANT: please note that the exchange charges a fee for deposits and withdrawals to the liquidity pool, and economicaly it makes sense to invest at least 5-10 TON in the liquidity pool for a period of at least 3 months, otherwise you may lose more on deposit and withdrawal fees than you gain. Ideally, you should invest at least 20 TON in the liquidity pool.

Blockchain

What is blockchain and the benefits of using this technology

Blockchain – is a method of secure storage and transmission of data in the form of a chain of blocks connected with each other by special keys, each of which contains information about the previous one. To change the information recorded in one of the blocks, it is necessary to correct all the subsequent blocks, otherwise the chain will be destroyed. It is impossible to do this covertly, so blockchain technology is considered a reliable way to protect information from editing and falsification. Thus, in simple words, blockchain is both a method of distributed data storage and a technology for its secure transmission at the same time.

Why using blockchain technology is optimal for this project?

– Full openness and transparency of all transactions, the inability to hide information. This is fundamentals of the Blockchain.

– Honest token trading conditions. Smart contracts allow the token to be programmed according to certain rules for greater trust of participants in the project. For example, the founders of the project cannot cheat anyone and withdraw funds (make rug pull), because 90% of the liquidity pool tokens are “burned” and the founders’ tokens are in vesting for two years.

– Decentralization. Blockchain gives you this opportunity. No one can block your tokens or take them from you. Your tokens are your property.

– Cross-border and global. This is a property of blockchain technology. There are no borders for transactions; residents of all countries where the token ownership is allowed can freely buy and sell the White Ray tokens.

– Self-organization from below based on everyone’s will. Blockchain allows to transfer the management of the project to its participants and make decisions based on the will of the project participants through voting.

– Affluence and abundance for all project participants. Blockchain opens access to global capital for new projects.
